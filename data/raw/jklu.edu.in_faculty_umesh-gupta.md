# Faculty

## Dr. Umesh Gupta

### Professor and Dean of Academic Affairs

#### Mathematics

Institute of Engineering & Technology, JK Lakshmipat University

## Biography

Dr. Umesh Gupta has 25 years of teaching and research experience. He is Professor in Mathematics and Dean of Academic Affairs at JK Lakshmipat University. His expertise includes statistical data analysis and optimization techniques. He has also worked as a Visiting Professor at St. Cloud State University, USA.

## Education

- Ph.D., University of Rajasthan, Jaipur, 2009. Topic: Studies in Problems of Steady and Unsteady Flows of Viscous Fluid.
- M.Phil., Mathematics, University of Rajasthan, Jaipur, 2002.
- M.Sc., Mathematics, University of Rajasthan, Jaipur, 2000.
- B.Sc., Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics, University of Rajasthan, Jaipur, 1998.

## Research Interests

- Statistical Data Analysis
- Optimization Techniques
- Education
- Fluid Mechanics
